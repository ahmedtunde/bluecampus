Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our video chat and messaging platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Email, username, and password when signing up.
  • Usage Data: IP addresses, device info, and session data.
  • Cookies: Session cookies to enhance user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the service: Managing accounts, video chats, and messaging.
  • Improve the service: Analyzing usage patterns.
  • Communicate with you: Sending important updates and notifications.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. However, we may share it with trusted third parties for essential services like hosting or legal purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your data, and opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes through email or the platform.

For more information, contact us at help@bluecampus.ng.
