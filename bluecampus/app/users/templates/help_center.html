Help Center

Welcome to the Help Center. Below, you’ll find answers to common questions and troubleshooting tips.

1. How to Create an Account

Follow these steps:

  • Go to the sign-up page.
  • Enter your email and create a password.
  • Verify your email through the OTP sent to you.

2. Joining a Video Chat

To join a video chat:

  • Log in to your account.
  • Select a room to join from the "Rooms" section.
  • Ensure your microphone and camera are enabled.

3. How to Send a Message

To send a message:

  • Enter the chat room and use the message box to type your message.
  • Hit send to deliver it instantly to all participants.

For additional help, please contact support at help@bluecampus.ng.
