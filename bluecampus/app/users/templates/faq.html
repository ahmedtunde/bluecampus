Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How can I reset my password?

You can reset your password by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login page. Follow the instructions to receive a reset OTP via email.

2. How do I update my profile?

Log in to your account, navigate to the profile settings, and update your details like username, email, and preferences.

3. How can I delete my account?

To delete your account, go to the account settings and select "Delete Account." This will mark your account as deleted.

4. Can I recover deleted messages?

No, once messages are deleted from the system, they cannot be recovered.

5. What devices are supported?

Our platform works on all modern browsers on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

If you have any other questions, feel free to contact us at help@bluecampus.ng.
